# 1.2 What Does AI Do?

## The short answer

Artificial intelligence machines (researchers often call them **“AI services”**) don’t think. **They calculate.**

They are among the newest, most sophisticated calculating machines in human history.

---

## What AI services actually do

Based on **data analysis**, they **make predictions**.

That is the core of most practical AI today:

| Step | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 1 | You give the system data (text, images, numbers, history, …) |
| 2 | The system has learned patterns from similar data |
| 3 | It calculates a prediction (next word, label, score, action suggestion, …) |

---

## Why this framing matters

It is easy to talk about AI as if it “understands” or “knows.” A clearer engineering view:

- No inner thoughts — **math over patterns in data**
- Outputs are **predictions**, not guaranteed truth
- Humans still decide how those predictions are used

This pairs with the idea from the introduction: AI **adds value to human judgment**; it does not replace it by magic.

---

## Key takeaway

**AI services calculate and predict from data.** Understanding that keeps expectations realistic when you build or use AI systems.
