# 1.7 How Is AI Evolving?

AI capability is often described along a spectrum: from systems that do **one job well**, to systems that handle **many related jobs**, toward the (still distant) idea of **human-level general intelligence**.

---

## Narrow AI

**Narrow AI** focuses on a **single task** — for example predicting your next purchase or helping plan your day.

- Scaling quickly in the consumer world
- Many common tasks and lots of data to train on
- Example: buy a book with a voice-based device

Most AI you use today (recommenders, assistants for one domain, spam filters, image classifiers) is **narrow**.

---

## Broad AI

**Broad AI** sits between Narrow and General AI.

- Not limited to a single tiny task
- More versatile — handles a **wider range of related tasks**
- Examples of newer broad systems: predict global weather, trace pandemics, help businesses forecast trends

Think “multi-purpose within a domain,” not “any human intellectual task.”

---

## General AI

**General AI** (sometimes called AGI) means machines that can perform **any intellectual task** a human can.

Today’s AI generally does **not**:

- Think abstractly like a person across all domains
- Strategize with human-like flexibility in every situation
- Use past experience to invent truly new products or respond with appropriate emotions the way humans do

**Don’t worry — AI is nowhere near full General AI.**

---

## Snapshot

| Level | Focus | Today |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Narrow AI** | One task | Everywhere (apps, devices, APIs) |
| **Broad AI** | Many related tasks | Growing (science, business, forecasting) |
| **General AI** | Any human intellectual task | Not here |

---

## Key takeaway

Current progress is mostly **Narrow** (and increasingly **Broad**) AI: strong calculators and predictors for defined jobs. **General AI** remains a research aspiration, not a present capability.
